Take the Voight-Kampff test online

This is a free browser game inspired by Blade Runner (1982). You are the subject. You sit across the desk in an interrogation room while the examiner describes short scenes and a machine watches your eye. There is nothing to download and no account to create, and a full test takes about five minutes.

How to play

  1. Press Enter the room. Sound starts here, so headphones help.
  2. The machine takes a baseline first: a resting reading before any question.
  3. The examiner describes seven short scenes. After each one, pick one of four answers with the 1–4 keys or by tapping. You have 15 seconds.
  4. Three screens show your readings as deviation from your baseline: pupil, flush, pulse, respiration and how long you took to answer.
  5. At the end the examiner stamps a report: human, replicant or undetermined. The machine itself never decides.

Subtitles are always on, and the settings include a reduced-motion option. The game works on desktop and mobile browsers.

Voight-Kampff test questions

In the film, the Voight-Kampff test is not a lie detector. The examiner asks emotionally loaded questions and watches for reactions the subject cannot control: the eye, a blush, the pulse, a pause before answering. This simulator works the same way. Each question is an everyday scene, often involving an animal, a child or a memory, and the four answers run from caring to cold. How quickly you answer counts too.

Every question was written for this game. None are taken from the film or the novel. Two examples from the simulator:

Simulator example · FoxNight. You're driving down an empty road. A fox darts into your headlights. You brake, but you still hear a dull thud. In the mirror, something moves by the roadside.

Simulator example · PhotographIn a drawer you find an old photograph. You're a child, holding a woman's hand in a garden. You remember neither the woman nor the garden.

Questions and answers

Is this the real Voight-Kampff test?

The Voight-Kampff test is fictional. This is an unofficial fan game that recreates the idea of it: empathy questions, involuntary reactions and an examiner who decides.

Why is it sometimes spelled Voigt-Kampff?

Philip K. Dick's novel Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? spells it Voigt-Kampff. The 1982 film uses Voight-Kampff.

Is this the baseline test from Blade Runner 2049?

No. The baseline test in Blade Runner 2049 is a different procedure, in which an officer repeats phrases under pressure. This game follows the original Voight-Kampff idea.

Can I play in Turkish?

Yes. The whole game is also available in Turkish: Voight-Kampff Testi.
