About · Blade Runner fan project

About this fan project

Voight-Kampff Test is a small, unofficial fan game by peekdock. It started from one wish: to sit in that chair from Blade Runner (1982) and be the one being tested.

Play the Voight-Kampff test

How it is made

  • The room and the machine are drawn in code and rendered live in the browser with three.js. The three-screen empathy analysis unit, the fan, the smoke and the haze are all part of one scene.
  • The sound is mostly synthesized with the Web Audio API: room tone, relays, bellows and the heartbeat.
  • The questions are original. None are taken from the film or the novel.
  • The interrogator's voice was generated with an AI text-to-speech model (VoxCPM2), running locally. Subtitles are always on.

What it is not

It is not a psychological test and it says nothing real about you. It is not affiliated with the film, the studios or the estate of Philip K. Dick. It recreates the idea of the Voight-Kampff test as a short piece of interactive fiction.

Play and guide

The game, the rules and example questions are on the main page: Voight-Kampff Test (with a How to play section below the title screen). It is also available in Turkish.
